GOVERDDHANA, author of the *Sapta S'ati*, or seven hundred miscellaneous stanzas, from which the idea of the *Sat Sai*, a similar collection in *Hindi*, may have been derived in the sixteenth century by BAHA'RILA'L, is the author of a vocabulary said to be still procurable in some parts of *India*: as he mentions the *Vrĭhatcat'há*, of which the antiquity is supposed to be about 700 years, and is thus noticed by ME'DINI'CARA, he probably flourished in the twelfth or thirteenth century.

24.
