The closing section of the *Médiní*, recapitulates the authorities employed in its composition, and comprises amongst others, the fullest list of *Cóshas* to be met with: on this account and as they appear to be enumerated according to their supposed comparative antiquity, I shall here transcribe the catalogue, accompanying it with such information as to their present existence, as I am able to offer from my own knowledge or from the oral accounts of the *Pan'd'its*:

1. The *Utpaliní*, is one of the supposed authorities of the *Amera Cósha*; it is certainly of considerably antiquity, and is often quoted by the older writers: it is still procurable* though not common.

2. The *Śabdárn'ava*, is often quoted by the commentators on *Amera*; it is also occasionally procurable, but usually in an imperfect state.

3. The *Sansáravertta*: a vocabulary often quoted in works of an early date, but supposed to be now lost.

4. The *Náma Málá*, though rare, is yet obtainable; as it is said to be a work of considerable extent, in that case it differs from the short work of DHANANJAYA of the same title, which I have consulted.†

5. BHÁGURI, is the author of a vocabulary, forming according to the commentators one of AMERA's authorities: the work is not to be met with.

6. VARARUCHI, is also considered as one of AMERA's originals although as one of the nine gems he should be contemporary with him: his vocabulary is not procurable.

7. The *Sáśwat*, is often cited, and is said to be procurable in a mutilated state.

8. VOPÁLITA, is the author of a *Cósha*, to be met with in scattered portions.

9. RANTIDÉVA, is the compiler of a vocabulary, no longer to be found in a complete state.

10. HARA, is another lexicographer, whose work no longer exists and who is rarely quoted.
