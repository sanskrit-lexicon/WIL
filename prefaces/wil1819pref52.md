# [ xlix ]

*(No heading is printed on this page beyond the bracketed page number — it is the continuation of the list of Authorities begun on the preceding page. Each entry below gives the abbreviation as printed at left, joined by dot leaders in the original to the full title at right.)*

Méd. = The *Médini Cósha*, the printed edition.
Muc. on Am. = The commentary on *Amera* of RÁYA MUCUT'A.
Náma. M. = The *Náma Málá* of DHANANJAYA.
Náná. R. Má. = The *Nánárt'ha Retna Málá* of ÍRUGAPA or DAN'D'Á'DI NÁT'HA.
Nár. on Am. = The commentary of NÁRÁYAN'A CHACRAVERTÍ on the *Amera Cósha*.
Níl. on Am. = NILACAN'T'HA's do.
Notes on Am. = Mr. COLEBROOKE's notes on do.
Or. D. = The original compilation, when the word contained appeared to be correct, and could not be found in any other authority.
Pad. Cau. on Am. = The *Padárt'ha Caumudí*, or commentary on the *Amera Cósha* so named.
Pán. = The *Sútras* or grammatical rules of PÁN'INI, the printed edition.
Rámá. on Am. = RÁMÁS'RAMA's commentary on the *Amera Cósha*.
Ramanat'h on Am. = RAMANÁT'HA's ditto dittto.
Ráj. Ni. = The *Rája Nighant'u*.
Ret. M. = The *Retna Málá*, the medical vocabulary so named.
Śabd. C. = The *Śabdárt'ha Calpateru*.
Śabd. Ch. = The *Śabdárt'ha Chandricá*.
Śabd. M. = The *Śabda Málá*.
Śabd. R. = The *Śabda Retnavalí*.
Śabd. S. Sin. = The *Śabda Sandherbha Sindhu*.
Sár. S. on Am. = The commentary of MATHURÉS'A on *Amera* entitled the *Sára Sunderí*.
Sid. Cau. = The *Siddhánta Caumudí*, the printed edition.
Sid. Cau. Uń. or S. C. Uń. = The *Un'ádi* chapter of the same grammar.
Swá. on Am. = CSHÍRA SWÁMÍ's commentary on the *Amera Cósha*.
Tri. = The *Trican'd'a S'es'ha*, the printed edition; the numbers refer to the section, chapter and verse.
Uń. Có. = The *Un'ádi Cósha*.
Vis. = The *Visva Pracásá*.
Vop. = VOPADEVA's *Dhátupát'ha*.
