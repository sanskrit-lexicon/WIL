# Explanation of the abbreviated References.

*Ajay.* = AJAYAPÁLA.
*Am. Có.* = *Amera Cósha*, *Serampore* edition with Mr. COLEBROOKE's translation.
*Anéc. Có.* = *Anécárt'ha Cósha*.
*As. Res.* or *A. R.* = *Asiatic Researches*.
*Bh.* or *Bha.* on *Am.* = BHARATA MALLA's commentary on the *Amera Cósha*.
*Bh. Dwi. Có.* = Do's———*Dwirúpa Cósha*.
*Bhá. D.* or *Díc.* on *Am.* = BHÁNU DÍCSHITA's commentary on the *Amera Cósha*: this is in fact the same as another authority or RÁMÁS'RAMA again specified below: there are also some other repetitions in the list, especially with regard to the *Commentators on Amera* arising from the different names used in different manuscripts of the same work, and my not adverting at the time to their identity in all other respects.
*Bháv. Pr.* = The *Bháva Prácása*.
*Bhú. Pr.* = The *Bhúri Prayoga*.
*Cau.* on *Am.* = The commentary on the *Amera Cósha* named *Padárt'ha Caumudí*.
*Cole.* on *Am.* = Mr. COLEBROOKE's notes to his translated *Amera Cósha*.
*Commrs.* on *Am.* = The different commentators as they were blended together in one manuscript.
*Dand'.* = DAND'ÁDI NAT'H the title of the nominal author of the *Nánárt'ha Retna Málá*.
*Dhan.* = DHANANJAYA the author of the *Náma Málá*.
*Dhar.* = The *Dharaní Cósha*.
*Dwi. Có.* = The *Dwirúpa Cósha* of BHARATA, &c.
*Éc. Có.* = The *Écácshara Cósha*.
*Hal.* = HALAYUDHA the author of the *Abhidhána Retna Málá*.
*Hár.* = *Hárávali*, the printed copy; the numerals indicate the verse.
*Hém.* = *Hémachandra*, printed copy; the numerals indicate the book and verse: the last six or alphabetical sections are designated as *Hém. Ná.* or the *Nánárth'a* chapters of HÉMACHANDRA.
*Jat'á.* = The vocabulary of JATÁDHARA.
*Mat'hu.* on *Am.* = MAT'HURÉS'A's commentary on the *Amera Cósha*.
