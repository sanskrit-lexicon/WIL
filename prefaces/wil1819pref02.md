*Honourable Sirs,*

I HAVE taken the liberty of dedicating to you the following *Sanscrit* and *English* Dictionary, as a public mark of the respect which I entertain for your Honourable Court, and as a public acknowledgement of those obligations which I owe to several of its individual members.

At the same time that I avail myself of this opportunity to gratify my personal feelings, I am satisfied that I shall find an apology for so doing, in the occasion that offers them to your notice, and that you will receive their expression favourably, in consideration of the work which they introduce to your attention.

_Dr. Mārcis Gasūns_
